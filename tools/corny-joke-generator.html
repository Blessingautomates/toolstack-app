Corny Joke Generator

Fifty-four jokes that are trying extremely hard to be nice to you. Puns, animal groaners and greetings-card humour, each with a groan rating on a five-block scale.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

What this tool does

Corny is a specific register and it is worth being precise about it, because corny and bad are not the same joke. A bad joke refuses to land. A corny joke lands exactly where it aimed, which is a soft target: it is wholesome, gentle, and would be completely at home on a greetings card. The groan is the applause.

The honest limitation is the flip side of the same quality. Fifty-four jokes that are all this nice are also all fairly similar in shape, and after a dozen you will be able to see the pun coming from a considerable distance. That is arguably the point, and it is still a real limit. There is no sharp material hiding further down the list.

How to use it

  1. Pick a style — animals, food, school, around the house, nature or everyday things.
  2. Press Tell me a joke and check the groan rating next to the badge. Five filled blocks is a joke operating at full power.
  3. Press again for another. Everything in the chosen style comes up once before anything repeats.
  4. Use Copy to put the joke on your clipboard and take it somewhere it can do some damage.

Common use cases

  • Making a child laugh

    The whole set is family-safe, and the puns are pitched at exactly the level where a seven-year-old finds them genuinely brilliant.

  • Being the person who tells jokes badly on purpose

    Corny jokes are at their best when delivered with total commitment and no apology.

  • Breaking up a serious meeting

    One groan is often enough to reset the room, which is a real and underrated skill.

  • Writing a card

    Copy a joke straight onto the clipboard and paste it into a birthday message that was otherwise going to say many happy returns.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a joke corny rather than just bad?

A corny joke lands where it aimed. It is wholesome and gentle, the kind of thing that would suit a greetings card. A bad joke is one that refuses to land at all, and this site has a separate generator for those.

What is the groan rating?

A hand-written score from one to five blocks for how much a pun hurts. It is a judgement call rather than a measurement, and it is part of the fun.

Is this safe to share with children?

Yes. Every joke in the set is clean and family-friendly, with no profanity, no adult content and nothing aimed at any group of people.

How many corny jokes does it have?

Fifty-four, across six styles with nine each. They are embedded in the page rather than fetched, so the tool is instant and works offline.
